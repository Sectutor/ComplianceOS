What Is NIS2?

The NIS2 Directive is the updated EU cybersecurity framework that expands coverage to more sectors and imposes stricter requirements on incident reporting, supply chain security, and risk management.

If your organization deploys AI systems in critical sectors - energy, transport, banking, healthcare, digital infrastructure - NIS2 applies to you.

NIS2 Requirements That Apply to AI

Incident reporting. AI system security incidents must be reported within 24 hours.

Supply chain security. If your AI system uses third-party models or APIs, you must assess and manage those supply chain risks.

Risk management. Documented risk analysis covering AI-specific threats like adversarial attacks and data poisoning.

Access control. Strict access controls for AI system administration interfaces.

NIS2 + AI Act Overlap

NIS2 and the EU AI Act overlap significantly. Both require risk management, incident reporting, and documentation. Organizations subject to both should implement a unified compliance framework.
