EU AI Act Compliance Checklist for AI Systems

A complete checklist of all 13 high-risk requirements under the EU AI Act. Free interactive assessment included.

📅 July 2026·📖 10 min read

The EU AI Act is the world's first comprehensive AI regulation. If your AI system operates in the EU market and is classified as high-risk, these 13 requirements apply to you.

"The EU AI Act does not tell you how to build AI. It tells you how to build AI responsibly. The requirements are framework-agnostic — they apply whether you're using GPT-4o, Claude, Llama, or a custom model." — European Commission, AI Office

The 13 High-Risk Requirements

Art. 9: Risk Management System

Do you have a documented risk management system for your high-risk AI system throughout its lifecycle? This is the foundation requirement — all others build on it.

Art. 10: Data and Data Governance

Are your training, validation, and testing datasets relevant, representative, and free from biases and errors? Data governance is a critical compliance area.

Art. 11: Technical Documentation

Do you maintain up-to-date technical documentation including design specifications and system architecture? This is required before placing the system on the market.

Art. 12: Record-Keeping

Does your AI system automatically log all events during operation, including user interactions and system decisions? Automatic logging is mandatory.

Art. 13: Transparency

Is your AI system designed to be transparent, providing clear and understandable information to users? Users must know they're interacting with AI.

Art. 14: Human Oversight

Do you have human oversight mechanisms that allow operators to override or stop the AI system when needed?

Art. 15: Accuracy, Robustness, Cybersecurity

Do you implement accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity measures appropriate to your AI system's risk level? This is where OWASP LLM controls intersect with the EU AI Act.

Art. 16–72: Conformity, Quality, Monitoring

Additional requirements cover conformity assessment (Art. 16), quality management (Art. 17), technical documentation maintenance (Art. 18), fundamental rights impact assessment (Art. 53), and post-market monitoring (Art. 72).

What Happens If You Don't Comply

Non-compliance with the EU AI Act carries significant penalties: up to €35 million or 7% of global annual turnover, whichever is higher. The Act is enforced by national market surveillance authorities, and serious incidents must be reported within 15 days.
