Why Enterprises Need AI Governance

Enterprises deploying AI at scale face a unique challenge: how to maintain control, compliance, and ethical standards across dozens of AI systems used by hundreds of employees.

An AI governance framework provides the structure for consistent decision-making about AI development, deployment, and monitoring.

Framework Components

Governance structure. Who makes decisions about AI? Executive sponsor, AI ethics board, risk committee.

Risk management. Integrated risk assessment process for all AI systems.

Compliance. Mapping to applicable regulations (EU AI Act, NIS2, DORA).

Ethics. Principles for responsible AI development and deployment.

Operations. Processes for model lifecycle management, monitoring, and incident response.

Technology. GRC platform, model registry, monitoring tools.

Implementation Roadmap

Month 1: Establish governance structure and risk appetite. Month 2: Inventory all AI systems. Month 3: Conduct initial risk assessments. Month 4: Implement GRC platform. Month 5: Deploy monitoring. Month 6: First governance review.
