Why Go Self-Hosted?

Self-hosted GRC platforms give you complete control over your compliance data, unlimited customization, and predictable one-time costs. For regulated enterprises, self-hosted is often the only option.

Key advantages include data sovereignty, customization, no vendor lock-in, and the ability to operate in air-gapped environments.

What to Look For

Control library breadth. Does it support NIST, ISO, SOC 2, EU AI Act, NIS2, DORA, and OWASP?

Automation. Automated evidence collection, risk scoring, and reporting.

API access. REST API for integration with your existing toolchain.

RBAC/SSO. Enterprise-grade access control.

Open source. Can you audit the code and contribute?

Deployment. Docker, Kubernetes, on-prem, or air-gapped.

Cost Comparison

SaaS GRC platforms typically cost $50-200 per user per month. For 100 users, that's $60,000-240,000 annually. Self-hosted platforms like ComplianceOS offer one-time licensing at a fraction of the cost.

Total cost of ownership over 3 years typically favors self-hosted by 3-5x for organizations with 50+ users.
