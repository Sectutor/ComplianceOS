What Is a FRIA?

Article 53 requires deployers of high-risk AI systems to conduct a Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA) before putting the system into use.

The FRIA examines the broader societal impact - potential discrimination, bias, privacy violations, and impacts on vulnerable groups.

When Is a FRIA Required?

A FRIA is required for high-risk AI systems used in law enforcement, border control, employment, education, credit scoring, and access to essential services.

If your AI agent makes decisions about hiring, promotions, loan approvals, or public services, you likely need a FRIA.

Template Sections

1. System description. What does the system do and what data does it process?

2. Rights identification. Which fundamental rights could be affected?

3. Risk analysis. Foreseeable impacts, likelihood, and severity.

4. Mitigation measures. Steps to prevent or minimize negative impacts.

5. Monitoring plan. Continuous monitoring for fundamental rights impacts.
