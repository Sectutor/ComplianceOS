## 🚀 The 8 Radical Sales Plays

### 1. Policy Review & Optimization (`/policy-review`)
- **Pitch**: "Expert Scrutiny." Stop using generic policies that fail audits.
- **Offer**: $800–$2,000 per policy set review.
- **Pain**: Outdated/fragmented policies blocking audit readiness.

### 2. MSP / vCISO Command Center (`/msp`)
- **Pitch**: "The Economic Disruption." Stop paying seat-taxes to Vanta.
- **Offer**: One Professional Seat for 50+ clients. One login. Total margin recapture.
- **Pain**: Vanishing margins due to per-client pricing models of legacy GRC platforms.

### 3. Compliance Kickstart Bundle (`/kickstart`)
- **Pitch**: "The Velocity." From zero to audit-ready in 14 days.
- **Offer**: $4,000–$12,000 End-to-end sprint (Policies + Gaps).
- **Pain**: Mid-market SaaS needs fast wins for milestones; they can't wait 6 months.

### 4. Gap Analysis & Readiness Sprint (`/gap-analysis`)
- **Pitch**: "The X-Ray." Stop guessing your readiness.
- **Offer**: $2,500–$7,500 (or $1k-$2k mini) 1-week guided sprint.
- **Pain**: Uncertainty. "Are we actually SOC2 compliant?"

### 5. AI RFP & Questionnaire Agent (`/rfp-agent`)
- **Pitch**: "The Accelerator." Stop losing deals to spreadsheets.
- **Offer**: AI Agent that drafts 300+ question responses in minutes.
- **Pain**: 150-300 question questionnaires blocking enterprise deals weekly.

### 6. AI Governance & Model Risk (`/ai-governance`)
- **Pitch**: "The Oversight." Audit the black box.
- **Offer**: Inventory + Bias/Risk Tiering for EU AI Act & ISO 42001.
- **Pain**: Emerging regulatory liability for AI-heavy SaaS.

### 7. Contract & Vendor Review Agent (`/contract-review`)
- **Pitch**: "Legal Liquidation." Stop waiting for legal to onboard vendors.
- **Offer**: AI Redlining of DPAs and SLAs based on internal policy benchmarks.
- **Pain**: Procurement bottlenecks delaying vendor onboarding.

### 8. Quantified Risk Intelligence (`/risk-management`)
- **Pitch**: "End the Heatmap Theater." Risk is a vector, not a color.
- **Offer**: $3,000 - $9,000 Baseline vs. Intelligent Register Automation.
- **Pain**: Subjective heatmaps that don't provide actionable data or financial impact.
