The "Cloud Scanner Trap" & Stage 1 Reality

Most companies attempting ISO 27001 certification purchase automated compliance scanners like Vanta or Drata. They connect their AWS, GCP, or GitHub accounts, resolve automated checks, and assume they are ready for certification.

Then comes the accredited Stage 1 Documentation Review.

The Auditor Reality
Accredited auditors (BSI, TÜV, SGS, Schellman) do not inspect cloud settings first. They open Clauses 4 through 10: Scope definition (4.3), ISO 27005 risk treatment (6.1), Statement of Applicability (6.1.3), Internal Audit reports (9.2), and signed Management Review minutes (9.3). Missing these causes immediate Stage 1 failure.

The 7 Pillars of ISMS Implementation

1

ISMS Scope & Context (Clauses 4 & 5)

Timeline: Weeks 1–2

Define the physical, cloud, and organizational perimeter of the Information Security Management System. Identify interested parties (customers requiring SOC 2/ISO, regulators like GDPR/NIS2) and publish the executive Security Policy signed by the CEO.

  • Clause 4.3: Explicit scope statement with justified exclusions.
  • Clause 4.2: Interested parties & legal obligations register.
  • Clause 5.2: Formal information security policy.
2

Asset Register & CIA Classification (Clause 8 / A.5.9)

Timeline: Weeks 2–3

You cannot assess risks on assets you have not cataloged. Register production VPCs, GitHub codebases, customer databases, and employee laptops with named owners and Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA 1–5) scores.

3

ISO 27005 Risk Assessment & Treatment (Clause 6.1)

Timeline: Weeks 3–4

Eliminate subjective heatmaps. Model concrete cyber threats (Ransomware, S3 Bucket exposure, Credential Stuffing, Vendor Outages) with Inherent Likelihood & Impact ($1\text{--}25$). Assign treatments: Mitigate, Transfer, Avoid, or Accept into an actionable Risk Treatment Plan (RTP).

4

Statement of Applicability — 93 Controls (Clause 6.1.3 & Annex A)

Timeline: Weeks 5–6

The master deliverable. Evaluate the 93 controls from the 2022 revision across 4 themes: Organizational (37), People (8), Physical (14), and Technological (34). Document formal business justifications for every applicable and excluded control.

5

Documented Information & Awareness (Clauses 7.2, 7.3, 7.5)

Timeline: Weeks 6–8

Deploy the 10 mandatory version-controlled policies (Access Control, Incident Response, Cryptography, SDLC, Backup). Collect 100% staff policy read-and-sign acknowledgments via an employee compliance portal.

6

Internal Audit & CAPA (Clauses 9.2 & 10.2)

Timeline: Weeks 9–10

Execute an independent internal audit across all clauses and active Annex A controls. Classify findings into Major Nonconformities, Minor Nonconformities, and OFIs. Perform Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and verify corrective actions before scheduling Stage 1.

7

Management Review & Clean Room (Clause 9.3 & Stage 1/2)

Timeline: Weeks 11–12

Convene C-level executives to review ISMS telemetry, risk results, and audit findings. Produce signed Management Review meeting minutes. Deliver the complete Audit Dossier to your external certification body.
