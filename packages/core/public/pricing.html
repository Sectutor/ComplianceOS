Frequently Asked

Is the Community edition really free? No hidden limits?
Yes. Community (AGPLv3) is fully functional — complete risk register, evidence management, framework mapping, policy lifecycle, BCP, audit workflows. No rate limits, no user caps, no artificial ceiling. The only limit is 3 client workspaces (vs unlimited in MSSP).
What happens if I stop paying for Pro?
Your premium features are disabled after a 7-day grace period. All Community features continue working. Your data stays on your infrastructure — nothing is locked or deleted. You can export everything at any time.
Can I self-host Community for free forever?
Yes. Deploy with Docker Compose on any VPS (Hetzner starts at €3.99/mo). No phone-home required for Community edition. PostgreSQL + your infra. Full backup/restore built in.
How does this compare to Vanta or Drata?
Vanta/Drata are SaaS-only — your compliance data sits on their US infrastructure. GRCompliance is self-hosted — your data never leaves your boundary. We also have BCP, federal, privacy center, and multi-tenant MSSP mode. Vanta has 375+ automated connectors; we're building ours. See the full comparison.
Can I use my own AI keys?
Yes. Pro tier lets you bring your own LLM keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, or any local endpoint). Your AI data never leaves your infra. Community edition doesn't include AI features.
What about EU data sovereignty?
GRCompliance is built for NIS2, DORA, and GDPR compliance out of the box. Self-host on Hetzner (Germany/Finnland), OVHcloud (France), or any EU provider. Evidence, risk data, and audit trails never leave your VPC. AI can run on local models. No data processing agreement needed — you're the only processor.
