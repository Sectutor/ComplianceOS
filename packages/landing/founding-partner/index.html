Founding Partner Questions

When does the founding offer expire?

The founding offer is available for the first 30 days after launch. Once the countdown reaches zero, all prices revert to standard rates. The countdown at the top of this page shows exactly how much time is left.

Is the founding discount locked in for life?

Yes. If you sign up during the founding window, your 20% discount is locked in for the lifetime of your active subscription. Even if you cancel and reactivate later, your founding rate is preserved.

Can I upgrade from Founding License to Audit Ready Sprint later?

Absolutely. You can start with the Founding License and upgrade to the Audit Ready Sprint or MSP Partner at any time. Your founding discount applies to the upgrade as well.

What if I'm not satisfied after signing up?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all self-host licenses. If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days, we'll refund your payment in full — no questions asked.
