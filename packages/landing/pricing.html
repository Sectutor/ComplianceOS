FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Consultants and Businesses pay the same base price?

We believe in simple, ethical pricing. Both editions use our battle-tested GRC software engine. Rather than charging arbitrary markups, we keep the base license identical ($1,990/year or $2,990 lifetime). Consultants get multi-tenant client workspace architecture and white-label reports; Businesses get unlimited internal seats, employee onboarding workflows, and SSO.

How does the 20% Founding Offer work?

To reward early adopters during our initial launch window (the first 30 days), all Yearly licenses are discounted from $1,990 to $1,592/yr, and Lifetime licenses are discounted from $2,990 to $2,392 one-time. If you choose the annual plan, your 20% founding rate is locked in for the lifetime of your active subscription.

What is the difference between Yearly and Lifetime?

Yearly ($1,990/yr or $1,592 founding): Lower initial cost, recurring annually. Includes 1 year of software updates, new framework additions, and email support.

Lifetime ($4,990 or $3,992 founding): Pay once and own your license key permanently. Includes lifetime software updates and support — no recurring fees, ever.

What is included in the Audit Ready Sprint add-on (+$5,510)?

The Audit Ready Sprint is our white-glove, 30-day intensive service. Our certified compliance experts conduct your gap assessment, draft all customized policies, configure automated evidence extraction, and run mock auditor interviews. Total cost is $7,500 ($1,990 base license + $5,510 sprint add-on). You walk away with both the software license and 100% audit readiness.

What does the Secure Install Service ($499) cover?

If your team does not want to manage Docker or cloud orchestration, our senior infrastructure engineers deploy ComplianceOS on your AWS, Azure, GCP, or private VPS. We set up SSL certificates, reverse proxies, automated encrypted database backups, and SSO/OIDC integration. It is a one-time $499 service fee applicable to any tier.

Where is our compliance and security data stored?

When using any self-host license, 100% of your data lives on your own infrastructure. Your confidential security policies, access logs, evidence files, and vendor assessments never touch our servers. You maintain complete sovereignty, compliance with data residency laws, and zero vendor lock-in.
