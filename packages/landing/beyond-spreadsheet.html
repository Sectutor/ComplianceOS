The Spreadsheet Trap

Small organizations and security teams often start with spreadsheets. It works... until it doesn't.

No Automation

Every update requires manual entry. Reminders? Due dates? You'll need another spreadsheet to track those.

Version Chaos

"Who has the latest version?" Multiple versions of truth. Conflicting data. Lost updates.

Can't Collaborate

One person editing at a time. No role-based access. No audit trail of who changed what.

No Reporting

Generating reports means copy-paste into PowerPoint. Executive summaries take hours.

No Integrations

Can't connect to your tools. Evidence has to be manually uploaded. No automated collection.

Audit Nightmares

Auditors ask for evidence from last quarter. You scramble to find the right version.
