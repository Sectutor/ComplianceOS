The fastest path to SOC 2 readiness.

You don’t need a $50K tool or a six-month consulting engagement to get SOC 2 ready. Here is the actual path teams use today.

Stop fixing spreadsheets.

Most compliance programs live in Google Sheets, Confluence, and Slack. That’s fine until the auditor asks for evidence of access reviews, policy versions, and risk treatments. By then, “fine” becomes “fragile.”

The proven path is not more documents. It is control-level organization: one place for policies, one place for evidence, one place for risk, with clear mappings to SOC 2 criteria.

What SOC 2 actually checks.

SOC 2 is built around criteria (security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, privacy). Auditors look for:

  • Policies that match real practices
  • Evidence tied to controls
  • A risk register with treatments
  • Vendor reviews for third-party services
  • Change records and access reviews

Build the program, then the Type II.

Start with SOC 2 Type I: a snapshot of controls at a point in time. That is faster and cheaper. Use it to prove you can operate the program. Then add continuous evidence collection for Type II.

Do not skip policy drafting. And do not let evidence be a manual weekly chore. Automate collection where you can, especially for cloud permissions, user access, and change tickets.

Open-source vs SaaS.

Self-hosted GRC gives you full control. You own the data, the schema, and the integrations. Managed SaaS removes infrastructure work. Both paths can reach auditor-ready outputs; choose based on control preference, not capability.
