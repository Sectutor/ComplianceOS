The Process

30 days. Four phases. Zero guesswork.

DAY 1-5

Discovery + Gap Analysis

We assess your current state against your target framework. Every control. Every gap. Every blocker. You get a complete readiness score with clear priorities. By Day 5, you'll know exactly where you stand.

DAY 6-12

Policy Writing + Control Mapping

We write your complete policy set — 15-20 policies mapped to your framework. Not generic templates. Customized to your stack, your team, your risk profile. Control mapping completes the picture.

DAY 13-25

Evidence Collection + Internal Audit

We configure automated evidence collection, then let it run. While evidence accumulates, we run an internal audit dry run — simulating what your auditor will do. Any gaps found get fixed immediately.

DAY 26-30

Remediation + Review + Handoff

Final gap remediation. Complete review of your compliance package. Team training on ComplianceOS. Auditor prep session. You walk away audit-ready with a living compliance program.
