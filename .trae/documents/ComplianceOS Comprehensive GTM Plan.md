## Product Summary
- Unified compliance operations platform for SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR: gap analysis, evidence, policies, risk, vendors, threat intel, AI advisor, posture forecasting.
- End-to-end lifecycle: CRM engagement stages → questionnaires → approvals → exports → continuous posture tracking.
- Differentiators: multi-framework mappings, auditor‑friendly exports, AI‑assisted controls, modern UX and fast time‑to‑value.

## Ideal Customer Profiles
- Tech‑driven startups/scaleups (25–250 employees) pursuing SOC 2/ISO quickly with limited GRC staff.
- Mid‑market companies (250–1,000) consolidating fragmented tools across risk, vendors, and audits.
- Consultancies/MSPs managing multiple client programs; need repeatable workflows and reporting.
- Security/compliance leaders (CISO, Director of Security, Compliance Manager) and Ops/Eng leaders.

## Core Value Propositions
- Accelerate readiness: AI‑assisted gap questionnaires and evidence automation reduce manual work 60–80%.
- Consolidate and control: risk, vendors, threat intel, and posture snapshots in one flow.
- Auditor confidence: exports and playbooks aligned to frameworks; multi‑framework mapping reduces duplicate effort.
- Continuous posture: forecasting and milestones drive predictability across audits.

## Messaging Pillars
- "From spreadsheets to outcomes": move from ad‑hoc docs to orchestrated program management.
- "Automation with oversight": combine integrations and AI with human approvals and audit‑ready outputs.
- "One platform, many frameworks": SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR with cross‑mapped controls.
- "Modern GRC for builders": fast, transparent, API‑friendly, integrates with Jira/Linear.

## Competitive Positioning
- Versus quick‑start tools: deeper workflows (CRM stages, public gap questionnaires, approvals) and multi‑framework mappings.
- Versus automation‑heavy suites: balanced automation and flexibility (BYOF imports like CIS/CCM) with faster time‑to‑value.
- Versus legacy GRC: modern UX, modular pricing, lower complexity.

## Pricing & Packaging
- Direct SaaS tiers: Starter ($12K), Growth ($24K), Pro ($48K), Enterprise custom; consultant tiers per managed client with volume discounts.
- Add‑ons: Vendor Risk Advanced, Threat Intel, AI Advisor/KB, Issue Tracker Sync, Cloud Asset Discovery.
- Pilot: 6‑month at 60% of annual tier; 15% annual prepay discount.
