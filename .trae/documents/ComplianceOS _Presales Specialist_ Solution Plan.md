# Upwork Service Offerings Powered by ComplianceOS

Based on the capabilities of your ComplianceOS platform, here are high-value service packages you can offer on Upwork. These leverage the software to automate 80% of the work, allowing you to price competitively while maintaining high margins.

## **1. "Audit Readiness" Packages (High Ticket)**

* **Title**: *ISO 27001 / SOC 2 Readiness Implementation & Policy Pack*

* **The Pitch**: "I will prepare your B2B SaaS for SOC 2 Type I or ISO 27001 audit in 4 weeks."

* **How ComplianceOS helps**:

  * Use **Policy Manager** to auto-generate the 20-30 required policies.

  * Use **Gap Analysis** to produce the "Statement of Applicability" (SoA).

  * Use **Readiness Wizard** to track progress.

* **Deliverable**: A fully populated ComplianceOS instance (or exported PDF reports) with all required policies and control definitions.

## **2. Fractional Security Officer (vCISO) Retainer**

* **Title**: *Fractional Security Officer: Risk Management & Compliance Oversight*

* **The Pitch**: "*Fractional Security Officer: Risk Management & Compliance Oversight*

* **How ComplianceOS helps**:

  * Use **Governance Workbench** to show monthly activity.

  * Use **Risk Register** to maintain the "Risk Log" (a constant auditor requirement).

  * Use **Dashboard** to send monthly "Security Health" PDF reports to the CEO.

* **Deliverable**: Monthly "Security Posture Report" and ongoing management of the platform.

## **3. Vendor Risk Management (TPRM) Service**

* **Title**: *Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment Service*

* **The Pitch**: "I will assess your critical vendors and provide a risk report for your procurement team."

* **How ComplianceOS helps**:

  * Use **Vendor Module** to log vendors.

  * Use **Scanner** (if active) or manual entry to log CVEs and security scores.

  * Use **Mitigation Plan Generator** to draft remediation steps for risky vendors.

* **Deliverable**: A "Vendor Risk Report" for their top 10-20 suppliers.
