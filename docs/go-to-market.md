# ComplianceOS Go-to-Market Plan (Phase 5)

**Tagline:** *Compliance infrastructure that stays on your infra.*

**One-liner:** ComplianceOS is the open source GRC platform you self-host — built for NIS2, DORA, and GDPR reality.

2. **Audit-grade exports** — Evidence packs with immutable audit trails
3. **EU regulatory alignment** — NIS2, DORA, GDPR frameworks built-in
4. **Self-host pricing** — No per-seat SaaS tax; pay for support and updates
