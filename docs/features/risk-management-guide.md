# Risk Management User Guide

## Overview
The Risk Management module in ComplianceOS provides a comprehensive framework for identifying, assessing, and mitigating organizational risks. It enables a proactive approach to security by linking risks directly to assets, threats, and compliance controls.

## Key Features

### 1. Risk Dashboard
The Risk Dashboard provides a high-level overview of your organization's risk posture.
*   **Key Statistics**: Monitor critical risks, overdue actions, and overall risk reduction.
*   **Heatmaps**: Visualize the distribution of risks based on likelihood and impact (Inherent vs. Residual).
*   **Risk Trends**: Track how your risk levels change over time as mitigation plans are implemented.

### 2. Risk Register
A centralized list of all identified risks.
*   **Filtering & Sorting**: Organize risks by category, owner, status, or severity.
*   **Quick Actions**: Update risk status or assign owners directly from the list.

### 3. Risk Assessment & Guided Workflow
ComplianceOS offers both standard and guided assessment workflows.
*   **Asset Identification**: Link risks to specific organizational assets (e.g., databases, applications, vendors).
*   **Threat Modeling**: Match risks against known threat vectors and vulnerabilities.
*   **Impact Analysis**: Evaluate the potential consequences (financial, operational, reputational) of a risk event.
*   **Likelihood Scoring**: Determine the probability of occurrence based on historical data and environment.

### 4. Mitigation & Remediation
*   **Mitigation Plans**: Define specific controls or actions to reduce risk likelihood or impact.
*   **Task Assignment**: Assign remediation tasks to specific team members with due dates.
*   **Tracking**: Monitor the progress of remediation efforts and verify control effectiveness.

## How to Conduct a Risk Assessment

1.  **Identify Assets**: Start by defining the assets that need protection.
2.  **Identify Threats**: Use the Threat Intel module or the guided workflow to identify potential threats to those assets.
3.  **Assess Inherent Risk**: Evaluate the risk without any controls in place.
4.  **Apply Controls**: Link the risk to existing compliance controls or create new mitigation tasks.
5.  **Calculate Residual Risk**: Determine the remaining risk level after controls are applied.
6.  **Continuous Monitoring**: Regularly review risks, especially after significant changes to the environment or threat landscape.

## Integrations
*   **Threat Intelligence**: Automatically pulls in relevant CVEs and threats based on your assets.
*   **Compliance Frameworks**: Links risks to controls from standards like SOC 2, ISO 27001, or NIST.
*   **Audit Hub**: Provides evidence of risk management activities for auditors.
