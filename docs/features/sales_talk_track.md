# ComplianceOS Sales Talk Track

## Who It’s For
- Tech‑driven teams seeking SOC 2/ISO readiness with real automation.
- Consultancies/MSPs managing multiple client programs.
- Mid‑market orgs needing risk, vendor, and threat intel in one flow.

## Pain We Solve
- Manual evidence collection and policy updates consume months.
- Fragmented tools for vendors, risk, and audits create duplication.
- Limited visibility into control posture and upcoming audit readiness.

## Why ComplianceOS
- Full lifecycle: CRM engagement stages from planning to certification and maintenance.
- Evidence and policies: Automation and exports tailored for auditor‑friendly packets.
- Gap questionnaires: Email‑based, public response, approve/apply workflows.
- Risk and vendors: Assessments, treatments, overdue analytics in one place.
- Threat intelligence: NVD/CISA KEV ingestion and asset–CVE matching.
- AI advisor: RAG KB, conversations, control tech suggestions.
- Posture snapshots and forecasting: Trend view and forward‑looking readiness.

## Competitive Positioning
- Compared to “quick start” tools:
  - Deeper workflows (gap questionnaires, CRM stages) with multi‑framework mappings.
- Compared to “automation‑heavy” tools:
  - Balanced approach: strong automation plus flexibility (BYOF imports: CIS, CCM).
- Compared to broader GRC suites:
  - Faster time‑to‑value with focused modules and modern UI.

## Pricing Narrative
- Tiers align to employee bands and framework needs:
  - Starter ($12K/yr): core automation for a single framework.
  - Growth ($24K/yr): adds risk/vendor and posture analytics.
  - Pro ($48K/yr): full stack including threat intel and AI advisor.
  - Enterprise: custom for complex multi‑BU needs.
- Consultant pricing per managed client with volume discounts for scale.
- Add‑ons for advanced vendor risk, threat intel, AI advisor, issue sync, cloud assets.

## ROI Bullets
- Reduce manual collection by 60–80% via integrations and automated evidence.
- Accelerate first audit readiness cycles (pilot in 6 months).
- Consolidate vendor, risk, and threat intel to cut tool sprawl and handoffs.
- Improve win rates with auditor‑friendly exports and continuous posture tracking.

## Demo Flow
- Navigate CRM engagement stages; show lifecycle visibility.
- Create gap questionnaire; send link; approve/apply responses.
- Review risk assessments and vendor analytics; surface overdue tasks.
- Show threat intel matches on assets; explain remediation paths.
- Open AI advisor; ask for control implementation suggestions; preview KB.
- Display posture snapshots; highlight forecast and milestones.

## Objection Handling
- “We need multi‑framework mappings.” Supported and automated; Pro offers unlimited.
- “We already use ticketing.” Integrations for Jira/Linear with remediation sync.
- “Our stack is unique.” BYOF and flexible integrations; manual fallback where needed.
- “Audits are complex.” Evidence exports and playbooks designed for auditors.
