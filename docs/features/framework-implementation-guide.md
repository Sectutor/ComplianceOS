# Framework Implementation Guide

## Overview
The Framework Implementation module is the heart of ComplianceOS. It allows you to adopt, manage, and prove compliance against various industry standards such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, and more.

## Key Components

### 1. Framework Library
*   **Standards Catalog**: Browse and adopt from a wide range of global compliance frameworks.
*   **Custom Frameworks**: Create your own internal control frameworks for specialized needs.
*   **Cross-Mapping**: Automatically see how a control in one framework satisfies requirements in another (e.g., SOC 2 to ISO 27001).

### 2. Readiness Wizard
A step-by-step assistant to launch your compliance journey.
*   **Scoping**: Define the boundaries of your assessment (which assets, locations, and departments are included).
*   **Gap Analysis**: Perform an initial self-assessment to identify areas of non-compliance.
*   **Baseline Selection**: Adopt a "Safe Baseline" or "Standard Implementation" to jumpstart your control mapping.

### 3. Control Workbench
Where you manage the implementation status of individual controls.
*   **Implementation Status**: Track progress (Not Started, In Progress, Implemented, Continuous).
*   **Control Owners**: Assign responsibility for maintaining each control.
*   **Evidence Linking**: Directly link documents, integration syncs, or autopilot runs to specific controls.

### 4. Harmonization
Eliminate duplicate work with Harmonized Controls.
*   **Common Controls**: Map one implementation to multiple requirements across different frameworks.
*   **Redundancy Reduction**: Update a control once and reflect compliance across your entire framework portfolio.

## Implementation Workflow

1.  **Select Framework**: Choose the standard you wish to implement from the Knowledge Base or Dashboard.
2.  **Run Readiness Assessment**: Use the Readiness Wizard to evaluate your current state.
3.  **Assign Ownership**: Delegate controls to the respective subject matter experts within your team.
4.  **Implement & Document**: Carry out the necessary security measures and document them in ComplianceOS.
5.  **Gather Evidence**: Use manual uploads or automated integrations to prove the control is functioning.
6.  **Verify**: Perform internal audits (using the Audit Hub) to ensure controls remain effective.

## Reporting & Exports
*   **Compliance Scorecard**: Real-time percentage completion across all frameworks.
*   **Gap Report**: Export a detailed list of what's missing for stakeholders.
*   **Statement of Applicability (SoA)**: Automatically generated for ISO 27001 and other relevant standards.
*   **Readiness Assessment Report**: comprehensive PDF documenting your compliance posture.
