# Knowledge Base & Advisor Copilot Guide

**Location:** `ComplianceOS > Knowledge Base`

The **Knowledge Base** is your centralized repository for compliance standards, technical documentation, and regulatory guidance. It is powered by the **Advisor Copilot**, an AI engine that helps you understand complex requirements and map them to your business operations.

---

## 1. Exploring Standards

The Knowledge Base contains a vast library of technical and regulatory standards:
*   **Security Frameworks**: ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIST CSF, CIS v8.
*   **Privacy Regulations**: GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, HIPAA.
*   **Industry Specific**: PCI DSS, SWIFT CSP, TISAX.

Click on any standard to view the full list of requirements, descriptions, and implementation advice.

---

## 2. Using the Advisor Copilot

The **Advisor Copilot** is available throughout the Knowledge Base (and on specific assessment pages). It can help you:
*   **Translate Requirement Language**: Ask "What does requirement 5.1 really mean in simple terms?"
*   **Implementation Advice**: "How should I implement MFA to satisfy both SOC 2 and ISO 27001?"
*   **Mapping Requests**: "Which ISO 27001 control covers laptop encryption?"

---

## 3. Bookmark & Quick Access

You can bookmark specific requirements or implementation guides for quick access. This is useful for building a "To-Do" list of technical controls you are currently investigating.

---

## 4. Federated Search

The global search bar in the Knowledge Base doesn't just search titles—it searches the full text of all indexed standards. Use it to find every mention of a specific technical topic (e.g., "Cryptography" or "Access Review") across all frameworks simultaneously.

---

## 5. Integration with Assurance

The real power of the Knowledge Base is its connection to the **Assurance** module. When you are performing a SAMM assessment or mapping controls, you can pull in guidance directly from the Knowledge Base without leaving your workflow.
