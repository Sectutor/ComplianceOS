# ComplianceOS Pricing Matrix

## Tiers (Direct SaaS)
- Starter — $12,000/year
  - Up to 75 employees
  - 1 framework (SOC 2 or ISO 27001)
  - Modules: Evidence, Policies, Gap Questionnaires, CRM Engagement Lifecycle
  - Support: Standard email support
  - Limits: 1 workspace, 10 integrations, 1 export pack/month
- Growth — $24,000/year
  - Up to 250 employees
  - 2 frameworks
  - Modules: Starter + Risk (assets, scenarios, assessments, treatments), Vendor Assessments, Posture Snapshots
  - Support: Priority support, onboarding workshop
  - Limits: 2 workspaces, 25 integrations, 3 export packs/month
- Pro — $48,000/year
  - Up to 750 employees
  - Unlimited frameworks
  - Modules: Growth + Threat Intelligence (NVD/CISA KEV ingestion, asset–CVE matching), AI Advisor (RAG KB, tech suggestions), Forecasting
  - Support: Priority support, success manager
  - Limits: 5 workspaces, 50 integrations, 6 export packs/month
- Enterprise — Custom
  - >750 employees or complex multi‑BU
  - All modules, custom SLAs, SSO/SCIM, advanced reporting
  - Limits: Negotiated

## Consultant/Agency Tiers (Per Managed Client)
- Starter — $4,000/client/year
  - Modules: Evidence, Policies, Gap Questionnaires
  - Limits: 1 framework per client
- Growth — $8,000/client/year
  - Modules: Starter + Risk, Vendor Assessments
  - Limits: 2 frameworks per client
- Pro — $15,000/client/year
  - Modules: Growth + Threat Intelligence, AI Advisor
  - Limits: Unlimited frameworks per client
- Volume Discounts
  - 10% for 10+ clients
  - 20% for 25+ clients
  - Custom for 50+ clients
