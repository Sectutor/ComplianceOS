# Policy Management Guide

**Location:** `ComplianceOS > Clients > [Client Name] > Governance > Policies`

The **Policy Management** module is where you define the "laws" of your organization. It supports the entire lifecycle of a policy—from creation and AI-driven drafting to distribution and employee attestation.

---

## 1. Creating Policies

There are three ways to start a policy in ComplianceOS:

*   **Policy Templates**: Choose from our library of pre-written, auditor-verified templates (e.g., Information Security Policy, Access Control Policy).
*   **AI Drafting**: Provide a few keywords (e.g., "Remote Work", "Laptop Encryption") and our AI will generate a comprehensive draft tailored to your organization.
*   **Custom Import**: Paste your existing policies into our **Rich Text Editor**.

---

## 2. The Policy Editor

Our editor is designed specifically for compliance documentation:

*   **Integrations Pane**: Link your policy to specific **Risks** it mitigates and **Controls** it enforces. This creates the "Golden Thread" of compliance that auditors love.
*   **Gap Analysis**: The editor provides real-time alerts if a policy covers high risks but lacks implemented controls.
*   **Version History**: Every save creates a timestamped version. You can restore previous versions or compare changes during an audit.

---

## 3. Publication & Distribution

Once a policy draft is finalized:

1.  **Publish**: Clicking "Publish" creates a locked, immutable version of the policy.
2.  **Distribute**: Send the policy to employees for review and acknowledgment.
3.  **Attestation Tracking**: Monitor who has "Read & Accepted" the policy in real-time. This data is automatically collected as evidence for your audit.

---

## 4. Key Workflows

### How to Prepare for an Audit?
1.  Ensure all policies are in **"Approved"** status.
2.  Verify that each policy is linked to at least one **Client Control**.
3.  Check the **Employees** tab to ensure you have 100% attestation for critical policies (e.g., Code of Conduct).
4.  Export the final versions as **PDF** to include in your Evidence Package.

### Handling Auditor Comments
Auditors can leave comments directly on specific policies in the **Preview** tab. Compliance teams should address these comments and update the policy before the final assessment.

---

## 5. Exports

*   **PDF**: Secure, tamper-proof format for final submissions.
*   **Word (.docx)**: Flexible format for external legal reviews or collaborative editing outside the platform.
