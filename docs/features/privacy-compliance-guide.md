# Privacy & GDPR Compliance User Guide

## Overview
The Privacy & Data Protection module in ComplianceOS is designed to help organizations meet the stringent requirements of GDPR, CCPA, and other global privacy regulations. This module centralizes your privacy program, from mapping personal data to managing individual rights requests.

## Key Features

### 1. Data Mapping (ROPA)
Create and maintain your "Record of Processing Activities" as required by GDPR Article 30.
*   **Inventory**: Document the flow of personal data across your business processes.
*   **Analyze**: Identify high-risk processing activities that require further assessment.
*   **Visualize**: Use data flow diagrams to understand where data resides.

### 2. Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA)
Systematically identify and minimize data protection risks for new projects.
*   **Assessment Workflow**: A guided questionnaire to evaluate necessity, proportionality, and risks.
*   **High-Risk Processing**: Automatically trigger DPIAs for activities like profiling or large-scale data processing.
*   **Consultation**: Document internal and DPO reviews.

### 3. Subject Access Requests (DSAR)
Manage and respond to requests from individuals exercising their data rights.
*   **Request Portal**: Receive and track DSARs (access, deletion, rectification).
*   **Workflow**: Verify identity, gather data, and generate response packages within statutory timeframes (e.g., 30 days).
*   **Secure Delivery**: Encrypted communication channels for sharing sensitive data with subjects.

### 4. Transfer Impact Assessments (TIA)
Evaluate the risks of transferring personal data to third countries (e.g., US, India).
*   **Schrems II Compliance**: Assess the surveillance laws and safeguards in the destination country.
*   **Supplementary Measures**: Document technical (encryption), contractual (SCCs), and organizational measures.

## How to Use

### Creating a Record of Processing Activity (ROPA)
1.  Navigate to **Privacy > ROPA**.
2.  Click **"Add Activity"**.
3.  **Define Purpose**: Describe *why* you are processing the data (e.g., "Employee Payroll").
4.  **Identify Data**: Select the categories of personal data (e.g., Contact Info, Financial Data) and data subjects (e.g., Employees).
5.  **Legal Basis**: Choose the lawful basis for processing (Consent, Contract, Legitimate Interest).
6.  **Recipients**: List internal teams and third-party vendors who access this data.
7.  **Retention**: Specify how long the data will be kept.

### Conducting a DPIA
1.  Navigate to **Privacy > DPIA Manager**.
2.  Start a **"New Assessment"**.
3.  **Screening**: Answer initial questions to determine if a full DPIA is required.
4.  **Description**: detailed description of the processing operations.
5.  **Risk Assessment**: evaluating the risks to the rights and freedoms of data subjects.
6.  **Mitigation**: Propose measures to address identified risks.
7.  **Approval**: Submit to the DPO for final sign-off.

### Managing a DSAR
1.  Navigate to **Privacy > DSAR Manager**.
2.  Log a new request received via email or form.
3.  **Verify Identity**: Confirm the requester is who they say they are.
4.  **Gather Information**: Coordinates with internal teams to locate the requested data.
5.  **Review & Redact**: Ensure no third-party data is accidentally disclosed.
6.  **Respond**: Generate the response letter and securely share the data package.

## Compliance Tips
*   **Embed Privacy by Design**: Start DPIAs early in the project lifecycle, not at the end.
*   **Keep ROPA Dynamic**: Review your processing records whenever a new tool or vendor is onboarded.
*   **Train Staff**: Ensure employees know how to recognize a DSAR and escalate it immediately.
