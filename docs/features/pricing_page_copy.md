## Frequently Asked Questions
- What defines an employee band?
  - Employee count includes full‑time, part‑time, and contractors with system access.
- How are frameworks counted?
  - A framework is SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, etc. Tiers include limits; Pro/Enterprise are unlimited.
- What is a workspace?
  - A workspace is a business unit or environment managed separately with dedicated settings and dashboards.
- Do add‑ons require Pro?
  - No. Add‑ons are available for any tier unless specified otherwise by technical requirements.
- How does onboarding work?
  - Starter includes standard onboarding; Growth adds a workshop; Pro includes a success manager.
- Can we switch tiers mid‑term?
  - Yes. Upgrades are prorated; downgrades take effect at renewal.
- Are audits included?
  - Audits are not included. We provide export packs, evidence automation, and auditor‑friendly reports.
- Do you support multi‑framework mappings?
  - Yes. ComplianceOS maps controls across frameworks and supports BYOF imports (CIS, CCM).
- What payment options are available?
  - Annual prepay or monthly billing. Enterprise offers custom terms.
