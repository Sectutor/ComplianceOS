# Employee Onboarding User Guide

## Overview
The Employee Onboarding module automates the process of communicating policies, training requirements, and compliance obligations to new and existing staff. It ensures that every employee understands their role in maintaining the organization's security posture.

## Key Features

### 1. Onboarding Dashboard
*   **Progress Tracking**: View the completion status of onboarding tasks for all employees.
*   **Reminder Automation**: Automatically send nudges to employees with pending tasks.
*   **Documentation Vault**: Access signed acknowledgments and training records.

### 2. Policy Acknowledgment
*   **Required Reading**: Assign specific policies (e.g., Acceptable Use, Information Security) to employees.
*   **Digital Sign-off**: Securely track when an employee has read and accepted a policy.
*   **Version Control**: Ensure employees are always acknowledging the latest approved version.

### 3. Compliance Training
*   **Curated Modules**: Integration with training platforms or internal training content.
*   **Completion Records**: Proof of training for audit readiness.

### 4. Background Checks & Verification
*   **Status Tracking**: Manage the status of background checks and identity verification.
*   **Integration**: Seamlessly link with external HR and verification services.

## For Administrators: Setting up Onboarding

1.  **Define Onboarding Groups**: Categorize employees (e.g., Engineering, Sales, Contractors) to assign relevant requirements.
2.  **Select Policies**: Choose which policies from the Policy Management module are mandatory for each group.
3.  **Configure Notifications**: Set up the frequency and tone of automated reminders.
4.  **Monitor Compliance**: Use the analytics view to identify bottlenecks in the onboarding process.

## For Employees: The Onboarding Experience

1.  **Welcome Email**: Employees receive a secure magic link to access their onboarding portal.
2.  **Task List**: A clear, prioritized list of documents to sign and training to complete.
3.  **Policy Editor Viewer**: A user-friendly interface for reading and acknowledging policies.
4.  **Achievement Tracking**: Employees can see their progress and download certificates of completion.

## Audit Readiness
The module maintains a full audit trail for every employee, including:
*   Timestamped policy acknowledgments.
*   Training completion logs.
*   Verification status history.
This data is automatically accessible in the **Audit Hub** during compliance reviews.
