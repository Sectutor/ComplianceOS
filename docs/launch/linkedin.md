## Headline
We open-sourced our GRC platform. Here's why — and what we kept for our paid tier.

## Body

Every week I talk to EU compliance teams frustrated with the same problem: their GRC tool is US-hosted SaaS, and under NIS2/DORA their evidence data needs to stay within their control.

So we open-sourced ComplianceOS.

**The Community edition is free (AGPLv3) and fully functional:**
✅ Controls & policy management with framework library (ISO 27001, NIS2, DORA, GDPR)
✅ Full risk register (ISO 31000)
✅ Evidence management with audit trail
✅ BCP/BIA (business continuity)
✅ Audit workflows
✅ Asset & vendor management
✅ Unlimited users. No rate limits. No hidden truncation.

**What's premium ($499/yr):**
🔷 AI drafting — generate policies, risks, and controls
🔷 Guided compliance wizard — "Get ISO 27001 in 30 Days"
🔷 Privacy center — ROPA, DSAR, DPIA automation
🔷 Federal modules — CMMC, FedRAMP, NIST 800-53
🔷 Trust center — public security portal
🔷 Advanced TPRM — automated vendor assessments, contracts, DPAs

**Why ComplianceOS vs Vanta/Drata:**
- Self-hosted → your data stays on your infra
- BCP/BIA → they don't have it
- Federal → they don't support it
- Multi-tenant MSSP mode → manage clients from one instance
- Cost: $0–$999/yr vs $10K–$80K/yr

I'd love your feedback — what's the one thing missing from open source GRC tooling?
